# Changelog

Newest first. Written for players.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24

The first release.

- You are Odd. The Matchers are everyone else. Stay odd for 10 minutes.
- 8 weapons with 5 levels each: Spark, Orbit Gears, Wrench-a-rang, Chain Lightning, Bolt Mines, Magnet Pulse, plus Odd Eye Beam and Hot Oil to unlock.
- 10 parts to bolt on: speed, hull, repair, pickup range, cooldown, damage, extra projectiles, area, armour and luck.
- 8 kinds of Matcher, gold-crowned elites that drop crates, two mid-run bosses (The Foreman, The Auditor) and The Template at 10:00.
- Beat The Template and you can keep going in endless mode, where it only gets worse.
- A workshop with 10 permanent upgrades, 3 extra loadouts to unlock, records and 9 achievements.
- Plays with keyboard, mouse or touch. Saves in your browser.
