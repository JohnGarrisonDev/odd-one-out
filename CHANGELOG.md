# Changelog

Newest first. Written for players.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-25

Tell us what you think.

- A new **Tell us** card under the game: a thumbs up or down and a few one-tap notes (too hard, too easy, broke on my phone...). Anonymous, no account, no typing.
- A Privacy link in the footer explains exactly what is counted.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-24

A balance update. Every weapon should now be worth taking, and the last fight should be fair whatever you built.

- **The Template sizes you up.** It arrives tougher if your build is hitting hard and softer if it isn't, then watches the first seconds of the fight and settles on a size so the fight lasts about a minute either way. A strong build also gets a Template that attacks a little more often. Its health stays within set limits, so it is never a pushover and never a wall.
- **Bolt Mines** hit less hard, with smaller blasts and a slightly slower drop. Still good, no longer the answer to everything.
- **Hot Oil** burns a little less and its puddles are a bit smaller.
- **Orbit Gears**, **Magnet Pulse** and **Wrench-a-rang** hit harder from level 3 up, and the gears orbit wider. Spark's top two levels hit a touch less hard.
- **Lucky Nut** does much more: more hearts, magnets, bombs and scrap, the odd free crate from ordinary Matchers, and a 4th upgrade card most of the time once it is maxed.
- **Endless mode** has a shape now: themed waves take turns every 45 seconds (Rush hour, Ram raid, Stamp duty, Bulk order, Everything at once), packs of gold-crowned elites arrive and grow, and an old boss comes back every two minutes, sized up to your build like the Template. The crowd still grows, but more slowly.
- Your workshop, loadouts, records and achievements carry over unchanged.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24

The first release.

- You are Odd. The Matchers are everyone else. Stay odd for 10 minutes.
- 8 weapons with 5 levels each: Spark, Orbit Gears, Wrench-a-rang, Chain Lightning, Bolt Mines, Magnet Pulse, plus Odd Eye Beam and Hot Oil to unlock.
- 10 parts to bolt on: speed, hull, repair, pickup range, cooldown, damage, extra projectiles, area, armour and luck.
- 8 kinds of Matcher, gold-crowned elites that drop crates, two mid-run bosses (The Foreman, The Auditor) and The Template at 10:00.
- Beat The Template and you can keep going in endless mode, where it only gets worse.
- A workshop with 10 permanent upgrades, 3 extra loadouts to unlock, records and 9 achievements.
- Plays with keyboard, mouse or touch. Saves in your browser.
